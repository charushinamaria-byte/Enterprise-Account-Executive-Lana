Prepare

Tips for your interviews

We're not looking for a polished performance — we want the real you. Here's how to show up at your best across all six stages.

Read about the product beforehand

Spend 30 minutes on creatio.com before every stage — explore the CRM and no-code Studio pages, watch a short demo video. You don't need to be an expert, just genuinely curious. Interviewers notice when candidates have done their homework.

Show your sales process

Prepare concrete examples from your enterprise experience — how you built a territory, managed a complex deal, or multi-threaded an account. Walk us through your thinking, not just the outcomes.

Bring real numbers

Revenue, quota attainment, deal sizes, win rates, ramp time — specifics make your stories land. We value commercial clarity and an ownership mindset. Show us you know your own metrics.

Prepare your questions

We always leave time for yours. Ask about territory structure, ramp expectations, competitive positioning, or team culture. Great questions signal great enterprise AEs.

For the case study

Think strategically — territory prioritisation, account entry strategy, stakeholder mapping, and how you'd close. Brief is shared in advance. Debrief is a conversation, not a test. The thinking matters most.

Be yourself

We hire experts and give them autonomy. We want to understand how you work, how you think, and whether there's genuine mutual fit. Authentic beats polished, every time.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Still have questions? Your recruiter is always happy to help — but here's what candidates ask most often.

Q
Is the role fully remote?
Yes — you can be based anywhere in the US. The role involves around 25% travel for customer meetings. If you're based in Boston, we'd love to see you at our Seaport District office — great view included. 🌊
Q
What does the OTE look like?
Commercial/Emerging Enterprise reps earn up to $300K OTE. Enterprise reps earn up to $350K OTE. Both tiers have a 50/50 split and uncapped commission. We have flexibility depending on how the conversation goes.
Q
How is the compensation plan structured?
The FY27 comp plan is built from two core components — ARR Bookings Commission (earned on your quota-bearing ARR bookings) and Quarterly Commission (for hitting your quarterly quota). On top of that, AEs can earn extra upside through 2x AI Commission, accelerators by quota attainment tier, a midterm bonus, and an assigned renewals bonus.
Q
What does the case study involve?
The case study is 60 minutes and has three parts: a Win Analysis (walk through a competitive deal you won — 15 min, 1–2 slides), a Loss Analysis (a deal you didn't win and what you'd do differently — 15 min, 1–2 slides), and a Roleplay (you're at a Digital Transformation Conference meeting a VP of Customer Experience from a regional bank — run discovery and pitch no-code BPM/CRM — 15 min), followed by a Q&A (15 min). Use real deals from your own experience. Preferably net new logo over expansion. You receive the full brief in advance.
Q
How long does the process take?
Typically 2–4 weeks from first call to offer. We move with intent — fast enough to respect your time, thorough enough to make a great mutual decision for both sides.
Q
How does the team perform on quota?
Creatio is ranked #1 on RepVue for comp rating and quota attainment. Over 70%+ of reps hit quota — consistently one of the strongest attainment rates in enterprise SaaS. We build territories and provide the support to help reps win.
Q
What are the deal sizes, quotas, and sales cycle?
Average deal sizes: Enterprise $150K–$500K ACV, Commercial $100K–$150K ACV. Annual quotas: Enterprise $1.2M, Emerging Enterprise $1M, Corporate $800K. Sales cycle runs 3–12 months depending on deal size and complexity. Key verticals include financial services, credit unions, manufacturing, public sector, and cross-industry accounts.
